About

National Seminar brings together researchers, practitioners, students, and stakeholders to discuss key issues in sustainability, digital transformation, and resilient development toward net-zero goals and positive community impact. Building Information Modeling Workshop

About

BIM Workshop equips participants with essential knowledge of Building Information Modeling (BIM) as a core component of digital construction. Beyond modeling, BIM serves as an integrated system that enhances collaboration and efficiency across project stages. Participants will gain hands-on experience with BIM and understand its importance in digital transformation, net-zero efforts, and sustainable, resilient infrastructure development.

Theme

"Driving Sustainable Construction with BIM Toward Net Zero Emissions"

Webinar SKK Konstruksi Jenjang 6

About

This webinar is a manifestation of the strategic synergy between CEIC and IKATEKSI, aimed at equipping final-year students and fresh graduates to face competition in the construction industry through preparation for Work Competency Certification. Furthermore, this webinar also aims to provide a platform and opportunity for SKK certification holders to earn SKPK points from LPJK. Designed as a preparatory session led by professionals, this event bridges the industry's need for professionals by enabling participants to understand the importance of PKB and access credible certification.

Theme

"Bridging the Gap: Transisi dari Mahasiswa menjadi Profesional Bersertifikat"
